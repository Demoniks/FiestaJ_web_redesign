Our Story

Santa Barbara's trusted family party rental company

At Fiesta Jumps, we believe great events create lifelong memories. For more than 26 years, our family-owned business has proudly served Santa Barbara and the Central Coast with one mission: provide safe, clean, reliable, and stress-free fun for families, schools, churches, nonprofits, companies, and community events.

What started as a small family business has grown into one of the most trusted party and event rental companies in the area. Through hard work, honesty, and dedication to our customers, Fiesta Jumps has become known for professional service, quality equipment, and a commitment to doing things the right way every time.

Every inflatable and rental item is carefully cleaned, sanitized, inspected, and prepared before each event because safety and quality always come first. Our goal is simple: allow you to enjoy your event while we handle the setup, operation, and details professionally and efficiently.

Over the years, we've proudly partnered with schools, nonprofits, churches, cities, festivals, corporations, and thousands of local families. At Fiesta Jumps, we are more than just a rental company. We are part of the community.

Thank you for supporting our family business for over 26 years. We look forward to helping make your next event fun, memorable, and stress-free.
